# Midjourney Prompt for "Вернулся из армии"

## Prompt:

a conceptual black and white photograph of an empty wheelchair in a deserted room. The wheelchair is starkly silhouetted against a large, bright window. The light coming through the window is harsh and fills the room with a sense of melancholic emptiness. The composition is minimalist and powerful. The style is inspired by conceptual photographers like Robert Frank and Josef Koudelka, capturing a feeling of post-war stillness and psychological weight. high contrast, grainy, emotionally charged. --ar 3:2

## Prompt 2 (with a person):

cinematic still, black and white photograph. A figure of a man in a wheelchair, seen from behind, as a dark silhouette against a blindingly bright window. The room is sparse, filled with a sense of emptiness and lost time. High contrast, heavy grain, emotional and raw. Inspired by the work of Robert Frank and Andrei Tarkovsky. --ar 16:9 --style raw
